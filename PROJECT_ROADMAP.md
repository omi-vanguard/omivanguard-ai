1. PROJECT VISION

Omi Vanguard AI will be an intelligent AI Agent that can understand natural-language instructions and directly perform spreadsheet tasks.

It will support Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets, from beginner-level operations to advanced professional workflows.

The AI must not only explain how to complete a task. It must perform the task, verify the result, and deliver the completed work.

Core workflow:

1. Understand the user's instruction.
2. Identify the spreadsheet and relevant data.
3. Analyze the task.
4. Create an execution plan.
5. Execute the required operations.
6. Verify the results.
7. Save and deliver the completed work.

13. COMPLETION CRITERIA

Omi Vanguard AI should progressively achieve these goals:

1. Perform basic Excel tasks directly.
2. Perform advanced Excel tasks where supported.
3. Perform basic Google Sheets tasks directly.
4. Perform advanced Google Sheets tasks where supported.
5. Understand Bengali and English instructions.
6. Execute multi-step spreadsheet tasks.
7. Create and modify formulas.
8. Clean and analyze spreadsheet data.
9. Generate professional charts and dashboards.
10. Create reports and finished files.
11. Verify results before reporting success.
12. Protect original data and user privacy.
13. Support future AI model providers.
14. Maintain a modular and maintainable architecture.

FINAL PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Omi Vanguard AI will become a powerful AI Agent for Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.

A user should be able to give an instruction in ordinary language and receive completed spreadsheet work—not merely advice about how to do it.

The primary development priority is comprehensive Excel and Google Sheets task execution, from basic operations to advanced professional capabilities.
